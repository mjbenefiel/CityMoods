Privacy

About you, the visitor

This site has no cookies, no accounts and no ads. To see which pages people read, we count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that sets no cookies and doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you. For each visit it records the page, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen size, and your rough country. When you open a neighborhood on a map, it counts that neighborhood's name too, and it counts clicks on the Shop designs and the tip button (the tip button is a plain link to Ko-fi; nothing loads from Ko-fi unless you click it). Your IP address is used to estimate the country and, mixed with your browser type into a scrambled code that is thrown away within a day, to tell new visits from repeat ones. It is never stored. Your browser loads everything else from this site only, except the optional address search on the Trends page (below).

Finding your neighborhood on Trends. If you tap "Use my location," your browser asks your permission and then works out your neighborhood on your own device, using the neighborhood boundaries this site already loads. Your location is never sent to CityMoods or anyone else. If you type an address instead, it goes only to OpenStreetMap's free address search (Nominatim) to find it on the map, and their privacy policy applies to that search. Either way, CityMoods never receives, counts or stores your location or address, and neither is ever added to the page's link.

About the people who called 311

New York City, Chicago, Los Angeles, San Francisco and Boston publish 311 service requests as open data. CityMoods uses that data carefully:

  • We download only the type of request, its problem details (chosen from the city's fixed menus, not typed freely), the agency it went to, and its rough location. We never download addresses, street names, property IDs, ZIP codes, request numbers or any written notes.
  • Each location is used once, to find which neighborhood the request came from, and is then discarded. It is never saved or published. In Chicago, Los Angeles, San Francisco and Boston, we don't download locations at all: each city already says which neighborhood or community area a request is in.
  • The site shows totals by neighborhood only. A neighborhood count under 3 is left out.
  • "Rare finds" (unusual request types) are shown for the whole city only, never by neighborhood.
  • Requests about people, such as homelessness, panhandling or police matters, are grouped into "everything else" and never named or featured.
  • Every daily build runs an automatic check that stops publishing if anything resembling an address or coordinate appears in the output.

The neighborhood totals behind the maps are free to download on the Data page.

The Shop

The Shop page is part of this site, but buying happens on Redbubble, which prints and ships the shirts. When you follow a link there, Redbubble's own privacy policy applies. CityMoods never sees your payment or shipping details.

CityMoods is independent and not affiliated with any of these cities.
