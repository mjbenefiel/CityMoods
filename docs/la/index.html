          How this works

          Every day, people send thousands of requests to 311, the city's non-emergency line, and the city publishes them as open data. Each night we group the latest full day of requests into a few moods and count them by neighborhood.

          Privacy: we never download addresses, names or written notes. Neighborhoods come from the city's own area codes or from a location used once and then thrown away. Only neighborhood totals appear here, and counts under 3 are left out. Requests about people, such as homelessness or someone's job, aren't shown or joked about.

          "Typical" means the average for the same weekday over the previous four weeks.

          Data: , from the city's open data portal. Weather: Open-Meteo. CityMoods is independent and not affiliated with the city.
