          How this works

          Every day, New Yorkers send around 10,000 requests to 311, the city's non-emergency line, and the city publishes them as open data. Each morning we group yesterday's requests into a few moods and count them by neighborhood.

          Privacy: we never download addresses, names or the free-text notes. We use each request's location once to find its neighborhood, then throw it away. Only neighborhood totals appear here, and counts under 3 are left out. Requests about people, such as homelessness, aren't shown or joked about.

          "Typical" means the average for the same weekday over the previous four weeks.

          Data: NYC 311 Service Requests and 2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Areas from NYC Open Data. Weather: Open-Meteo. CityMood is independent and not affiliated with the City of New York.
